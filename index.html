💸 Monte Carlo Simulation of Cash Waqf-Linked Sukuk (CWLS) for MBG

This project applies a Monte Carlo–based stochastic financial simulation to assess the long-term sustainability of a perpetual waqf fund. Over a 10-year horizon with 10,000 simulated paths, the model incorporates randomly generated investment returns and food inflation rates to project MBG cost dynamics and fund performance. The simulation framework enables risk assessment, scenario evaluation, and probabilistic estimation of fund adequacy under economic uncertainty.

Tools: R, Python, ggplot2, statistical modeling
